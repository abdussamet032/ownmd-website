FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is OwnMD free?

Yes. OwnMD is free to download and use.

Does OwnMD support KaTeX or math?

Yes. OwnMD renders KaTeX math expressions in your Markdown documents.

Can I create diagrams?

Yes. Mermaid diagrams are supported for flowcharts, sequence diagrams, and more.

Is OwnMD open source?

Yes. The project is open source and available on GitHub.

Which languages are supported?

OwnMD includes multiple interface languages including English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Portuguese, and Chinese.
